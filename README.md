## 🚀 My Projects

### 💼 NaukriHub – Job Portal Website

**NaukriHub** is a job portal website designed to connect **employers and job seekers** through a single platform.

#### Key Features:

* 👨‍💼 Separate access for Employers and Job Seekers
* 🔐 User Registration and Login
* 📋 Job Posting and Job Searching
* 👤 Job Seeker Profile Management
* 🏢 Employer Profile Management
* 🔍 Easy Job Search and Application Process

The main objective of NaukriHub is to simplify the recruitment process and provide an easy platform where employers can post job opportunities and job seekers can explore and apply for suitable jobs.
